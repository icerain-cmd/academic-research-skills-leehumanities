The nested plugin is self-contained. It does not install the upstream plugin's hooks, invoke external model APIs, or inherit all of upstream ARS's claimed capabilities. Evidence-record checks are mechanical conformance checks; they do not certify the truth, novelty, completeness, or publication readiness of a manuscript. Four isolated Claude roles are not four independent model families.

Source papers, unpublished manuscripts, user credentials, local corpus locations and real review ledgers are not distributed in this package. Runtime artifacts belong to a user-selected research project outside the installed plugin.
